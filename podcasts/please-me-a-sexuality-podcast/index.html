Podcast with EveWhere Sexuality, Self Improvement, and Education Take Center Stage Please Me! is a bold, sex-positive, body-positive podcast exploring sexuality, sexual health, intimacy, and empowerment—without shame or taboos.

Hosted by Eve Hall, a licensed Physical Therapist specializing in pelvic floor therapy and sexual wellness, this show is your no-BS guide to reclaiming desire, closing the orgasm gap, and embracing radical self-love and authentic sexuality. Through candid conversations, expert interviews, and unfiltered real talk, Please Me! explores what it truly means to thrive erotically, emotionally, and physically.
