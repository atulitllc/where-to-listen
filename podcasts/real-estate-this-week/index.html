Looking for the best real estate podcast to help you buy, sell, or invest in property? Join Stephen Gasque—real estate expert and five-time Emmy Award–winning broadcast journalist—as he and his expert guests share the latest real estate tips, mortgage strategies, and property investing insights.

With over 16 years on radio and online, Stephen combines his two careers—real estate and broadcasting—to bring you clear, practical advice on today's housing market. Each week, Real Estate This Week dives into buying a home, selling real estate, mortgage refinancing, first-time homebuyer tips, and smart real estate investing.
