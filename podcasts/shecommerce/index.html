Bold Brands. Fierce Women. One Sisterhood. SHeCommerce is more than just a podcast, it's a community.

Hosted by Cristina and Jax, two seasoned CPG leaders with almost 50 years of combined international experience, SHeCommerce dives deep into the heart of the CPG revolution, tackling the issues that matter most to women in the industry. Tired of surface-level business podcasts? We get it. That's why we're committed to providing real talk, real solutions, and a whole lot of heart. From decoding the latest retail media strategies and accelerating omni-commerce growth to navigating work-life imbalance, shattering stereotypes, and fighting for wage equality – nothing is off-limits.
