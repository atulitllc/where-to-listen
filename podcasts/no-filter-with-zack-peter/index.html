No PR spin. No sacred cows. No apologies. Welcome to the No Filter with Zack Peter podcast where celebrities get called out, cultural controversies get dissected, and the takes are hotter than the headlines.

Hosted by Zack Peter, this is where pop culture collides with the culture wars—and nothing is too messy, too controversial, or too politically incorrect to discuss. From Hollywood scandals and celebrity feuds to reality TV chaos, cancel culture, true crime, and the internet's latest obsession, Zack brings fearless opinions, sharp wit, and zero interest in playing it safe. Think you know the story? Think again.
