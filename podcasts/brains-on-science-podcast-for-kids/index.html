Brains On!® is a science podcast for curious kids and adults from Brains On Universe. Each week, a different kid co-host joins Molly Bloom to find answers to fascinating questions about the world sent in by listeners.

Like, do dogs know they’re dogs? Plus, we have mystery sounds for you to guess, songs for you to dance to, and lots of facts -- all checked by experts.
