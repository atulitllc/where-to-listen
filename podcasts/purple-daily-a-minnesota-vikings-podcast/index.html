The number one Minnesota Vikings podcast in America, with one mission: We want the Vikings to win the Super Bowl before we die! The Purple Daily YouTube channel specializes in DAILY, entertaining, insightful discussions about the Minnesota Vikings - with shows like Purple Daily, Vikings Vent Line, Purple Access and more.

Phil Mackey and Judd Zulgad are long-time radio hosts in the Twin Cities and former Vikings beat writers. Other regular contributors include former Minnesota Vikings offensive linemen (and hosts of The OLine Committee) Alex Boone and Jeremiah Sirles; Thor Nystrom from Thor Talks Purple; Plus, executive producer and often-mustached Declan Goff.
