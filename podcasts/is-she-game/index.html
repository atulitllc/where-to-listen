"Is She Game?" is a hub for the ever-expanding, culture-shaping world of women’s sports – building community by serving fans and creating new ones. Each week, host Coach Jackie J breaks down this week’s news in sports and culture, hangs out with a special guest, and sets you up with moments to look forward to in the week to come. Jackie is the People’s Coach: teaching you all about women’s sports, so YOU TOO can have the time of your life as a fan of whatever sport catches your eye!

From the WNBA to the rec rugby league your mom just joined, she has her finger on the pulse of sports worldwide. She can’t wait to turn you into a women’s sports fan. We love women’s sports!
