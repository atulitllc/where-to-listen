Celebrity interviews mixed with personal asides from Shepard and Monica Padman. Hi, I’m Dax Shepard, and I love talking to people.

I invite you to join me as I explore other people’s stories. We will celebrate, above all, the challenges and setbacks that ultimately lead to growth and betterment.
