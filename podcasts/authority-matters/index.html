Authority Matters is a show about how trust, attention, and authority are changing online. Hosted by The Legal Podcast Network and the Authority Podcast Network featuring Dennis "DM" Meador, the series explores branding, media, marketing, audience building, and business growth in a post-corporate world.

Each episode breaks down why traditional trust is eroding, why visibility has become a competitive advantage, and how founder-led media, personal branding, and audience ecosystems are reshaping the way professionals and businesses grow. This isn’t about becoming an influencer. It’s about becoming known before you’re needed.
